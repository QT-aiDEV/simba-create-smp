# Simba Drippy Loading Art Brief

## Goal

Create a polished Simba Create SMP startup identity for both NeoForge's early-loading phase and Minecraft's normal resource-loading screen.

## Requested Assets

-- Primary Simba logo: transparent PNG, ideally 1600×600 or larger.
-- Loading background: 16:9 PNG at 2560×1440, with a safe center area for the logo and progress display.
-- Optional alternate background for the early-loading screen, also 2560×1440.
-- Small square emblem/icon: transparent PNG at 512×512.
-- Optional foreground frame or corner machinery: transparent 2560×1440 PNG.

## Visual Direction

Industrial Create machinery, brass/copper/andesite, rail or factory silhouettes, and subtle underwater/airship references. Keep the middle readable and avoid high-detail art directly behind progress text.

## Layout Targets

-- Logo centered in the upper-middle safe area.
-- Progress bar near the lower third with strong contrast.
-- Small build label: `Simba Create SMP 0.10.2`.
-- Avoid flashing, rapid animation and large uncompressed image sequences.

## Delivery

Provide layered source files plus exported PNGs. Do not bake progress text or bars into the background; Drippy will render those elements responsively.
