## Substitutions

- Haven Trowel replaces Trowels Fork because Trowels Fork stops at Forge 1.20.3.
- Bad Mobs replaces You Shall Not Spawn because YSNS has no compatible NeoForge 1.21.1 file. Natural creeper, skeleton/stray/bogged, spider, and cave-spider spawning is disabled; spawners and eggs still work.

## Not added

- Immersive Vehicles: the base framework contains no vehicles without separate content packs and overlaps the pack's Create/Aeronautics transport identity.
- Generic weapon mods: no specific mod was named; Create Big Cannons supplies the requested weapon-engineering lane without making an arbitrary gun-pack choice.
- Rechiseled: Chipped: no separate addon by that combined name was identified; Chipped, Rechiseled, and Rechiseled: Create are already installed.

## Player-test flags

- Parcool movement feel and keybind overlap.
- Iris camera capture behavior in Exposure.
- Big Cannons plus Radars gameplay balance.
- Horde frequency and zombie difficulty after the seven-day grace period.
- Immersive Aircraft overlap with build-your-own Aeronautics vehicles.
