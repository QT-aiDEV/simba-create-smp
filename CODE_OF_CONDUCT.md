# Community Code of Conduct

We want Simba Create SMP to be a welcoming place for builders, explorers, server owners, mod authors, and first-time contributors.

Be respectful, assume good faith, give actionable feedback, and keep disagreements focused on the pack rather than the person. Harassment, hate speech, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Respect mod authors and their licenses. Do not pressure authors for updates, repost their work without permission, or use this community to coordinate harassment.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Serious or sensitive conduct reports should be sent privately to the repository owner rather than posted as a public issue.
